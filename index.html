Features

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

Built for real parking-lot problems — lights left on, a blocked driveway, a flat tyre — and for the rare emergency.

Privacy first

No phone number on your windshield. Neither side ever sees the other's number.

No app for the sender

Whoever scans the card picks a language and one of 18 ready sentences in 5 categories. No sign-up, no account.

Instant alerts, your language

A push notification in your own language, naming your vehicle and plate — urgent ones come with a loud alert.

One-tap reply

Answer with a ready sentence and the sender sees it on the same page. No free text, no arguments.

Alert helpers

If you don't answer in time, up to two people you trust get the alert. Only urgent alerts escalate.

Smart reminders

Third-party and body insurance, technical inspection, oil change and service — a quiet nudge before each is due.

Many vehicles, one app

Add every car you own. Selling one? Release the card and the new owner claims it by scanning the same code.

8 languages, 28 countries

Persian, English, Arabic, Turkish, Kurdish (Sorani), Azerbaijani, Turkmen and Armenian — right-to-left included.

Optional · Emergency mode

When it's more than a parking problem

A bystander can raise an emergency from the scan page. You are alerted first; if you don't respond within a short window, your two close contacts are reached. Your medical card is opt-in, encrypted, and every view is logged.

How it works

Three steps. Zero phone numbers.

  1. 1

    Put the card on your car

    Stick the CarPing card on the windshield or dashboard and register it once in the app by scanning its code.

  2. 2

    Someone scans it

    A web page opens — no app needed. They choose a language and a ready message, like "lights left on".

  3. 3

    You get the alert

    Your phone buzzes with the message and your vehicle's name. Reply with one tap — your number stays private.

Support

Questions, answered

Do I have to give my phone number?

No. The app creates your owner record silently on first start — there is no sign-up, login or password, and the person who scans never sees your number.

Does the person who scans need the app?

No. They open a web page in their own language, pick a ready sentence and send it. That's all.

What if the card is lost or stolen?

Suspend it from the app in one tap, activate it again if you find it, or replace it with a new card. A revoked card stops working for good.

I'm selling my car. What happens to the card?

Release it in the app. The new owner scans the same code and claims it. Nothing needs to be re-printed.

What does emergency mode share?

Nothing, unless you turn it on. When you do, your medical card and two close contacts are stored encrypted, you are always alerted first, and every view of your data is logged.

Which countries and languages are supported?

28 countries — the Arab League states plus Iran, Türkiye, Afghanistan, Azerbaijan, Armenia and Turkmenistan — and 8 languages.
